{%- comment -%} Sierpinski triangle, after lutanho.net/fractal/sierpa.html: pointing down, text colour on the page background, zooming forever into its bottom point. Each level is three half-size copies of the level below (sier-1 to sier-8, 8 levels). Zooming by 2 about the bottom point turns the bottom copy into the whole, so the loop is seamless. The triangle is as tall as it is wide so it fills the square tile. {%- endcomment -%}
