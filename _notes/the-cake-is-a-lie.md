---
title: "The cake is a lie"
date: 2026-10-16
---

The cake on my home page stands for three things.

**Portal 2.** It's the game that first got me thinking about games as art. It was probably the first one I actually paid attention to: not just immersed in, but fascinated by. The story, the corporate satire, the humor, and the cynicism all hit preteen and teenage me hard, and it was the first time a game made me think seriously about what it was trying to say.

**The lie.** In the series, GLaDOS keeps Chell moving through test chambers by promising cake at the end. There is no cake. It's a pretty apt metaphor for America, or the rat race in general: keep solving the puzzles, keep passing the tests, and the reward you were promised was never real.

**The ride.** It also reminds me of Bill Hicks's "It's Just a Ride." His point is that the world is like a ride at an amusement park: loud, brightly colored, and fun for a while, and so convincing that we forget it's a ride we chose to get on. Because it's just a ride, we can change it whenever we want. It's only a choice, between fear and love.

So the cake is a lie, and that's fine. Knowing the cake isn't real is what frees you to choose a better ride.
