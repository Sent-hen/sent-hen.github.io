---
title: "The cake is a lie"
date: 2026-10-16
---

The cake on my home page comes from Portal 2, the game that introduced me to art. It was probably the first game I really paid attention to: not just immersed in, but fascinated by. Its story, its corporate satire, and its mix of humor and cynicism resonated deeply with preteen and teenage me, and it was the first time a game made me think hard about what it had to say. In the game, GLaDOS keeps Chell moving from one test to the next with the promise of cake at the end, but the cake isn't real. It's also an apt metaphor for America, or the rat race: keep solving the puzzles, keep passing the tests, and the reward you were promised was never there.

The lie also brings to mind Bill Hicks's "It's Just a Ride." Hicks compares the world to a ride at an amusement park: loud, brightly colored, and fun for a while, and so convincing that we forget we chose to get on. The people most invested in the ride don't want to hear that it's just a ride. But because it is one, we can change it whenever we want; it's only a choice, between fear and love. So the cake is a lie, and that's fine. Knowing it isn't real is what lets you choose a better ride.
