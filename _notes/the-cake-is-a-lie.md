---
title: "The cake is a lie"
date: 2026-10-16
---

The cake on my home page is about three things.

**Portal 2.** It's the game that introduced me to art. It was probably the first game I really paid attention to: not just immersed in, but fascinated by. The story, the corporate satire, the humor and the cynicism resonated deeply with teenage and preteen me, and it was the first time a game made me think hard about what it was saying.

**The lie.** In the series, GLaDOS keeps Chell moving through test chambers with the promise of cake at the end. There is no cake. It's also an apt metaphor for America, or the rat race: keep solving the puzzles, keep passing the tests, and the reward you were promised was never real.

**The ride.** It also makes me think of Bill Hicks's "It's Just a Ride." His point is that the world is like a ride at an amusement park: loud, brightly colored, and fun for a while, so convincing that we forget it's a ride we chose to get on. And because it's a ride, we can change it any time we want. It's only a choice, between fear and love.

So the cake is a lie, and that's fine. Knowing the cake isn't real is what lets you choose a better ride.
