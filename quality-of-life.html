Quality of Life

Quality of life is much like an oxymoron (paradoxical notion)

As individuals we want a harmony of sorts between things we think are opposites but are more so different sides of the same coin

  • Money and Time
  • Order and Chaos
  • Life and Death

These are things the mind and matter can feel directly

There are many cultures that come to this point of an escape from this ouroboros of a snake eating its own tail, of consumption and excretion

  • Nirvana
  • Samsara
  • Moksha
  • Rebirth

These are usually the extreme outlooks on how to achieve the ultimate quality of life shall we say, individually

But there is a much easier way, but it requires others.

Which is teaching and connection, and experiencing vicarious enjoyment through the eyes of an amateur/virgin, where they go through the same process you did while learning it.

Ultimately I think that life can be encapsulated in a coin flip. It usually lands heads or tails, but since we live in a 3D world, it has a chance of landing on its edge.

I guess this framework seems to match my thinking
